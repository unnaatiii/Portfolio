This reading involves rise & fall of emotions

1. This is the story about the first workplace Award.

Sometimes God just sits there like: ‘Let’s add a little drama for character development.’” 😭 And then at the last second, it winks 😏 and prove "JK! I'm right here, my child"

I faced this when our company's award ceremony was in full swing. Department by department, 
our founder took the stage to present awards. Marketing done. Tech team done. My team... 

And after every name, my brain was like:

“Okay maybe next.”
“Yeah definitely next.”
“Bro at least a certificate?” 💀

And then suddenly…

The anchors moved to the HR department.

I did the math. Quietly. Calmly. I didn't get it.
The ceremony wrapped up. The founder stepped off stage. People clapped. I clapped. Good vibes all around on the outside.

Then the anchors came back on the mic.
"We sincerely apologize, we missed presenting one award."

A pause. EXCUSE ME WHAT???

Founder called back on stage.

And then..........

They announced my name 😭

I’m telling you, the emotional transition from:
 “I didn’t get it”
 to
 “OMG WHAT, I GOT IT”

One minute I'm composing a very mature internal monologue about growth and patience and even that 
"You don't need an award to prove you work hard!". 
The next, WALKING TO THE STAGE holding an award I didn't think was coming.

From disappointment--> little heartbreak --> confusion --> hope --> shock --> being on cloud nine, all under 60 seconds 😭

Sometimes, the universe waits for you to accept the disappointment…
just to make the comeback 10x more cinematic. ✨

Moral of the story:
Never trust the plot before the episode ends 😭✨
